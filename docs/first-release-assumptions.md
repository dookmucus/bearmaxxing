# First-release calculation boundaries

BearMaxxing provides one estimated hosting team, three simultaneous joining squads and supported next upgrades. It is a planning aid, not an absolute damage forecast or a claim of independently verified game mechanics.

- Inputs come from the complete current profile: heroes and explicit skills, active class gear, Masters, Pets, troop inventory, tiers/Truegold and entered formation. Entered progression is never replaced with maximum values. When a skill has no explicit value, its documented unlocked maximum is derived from entered stars and recorded as assumed. Unknown offensive values remain unknown.
- Only the 12 primary class/slot gear IDs are active. Their class assignment transfers to the selected host; archived/imported pieces do not provide a second equipment set. Heroes cannot overlap between simultaneous squads. A joining leader goes first; fillers complete classes, with equivalent capacity treated as a tie. Personal capacity already included in the entered maximum is not added again.
- Host estimates include separate hypothetical incoming contexts containing four **selected** skills from other players. The representative mix is Chenko, Yeonwoo and two Amane at primary skill level 5; other level/mix cases test sensitivity. These are not attendance predictions or probabilities. The player's outgoing squads enter separate rallies and never supply incoming host effects.
- Same-family skill bonuses add and distinct families multiply using published controlled community tests. Applying those PvP families to Bear is a transfer assumption. Incoming Vivian duplicate/replacement overlap remains unresolved. Other retained uncertainties include exact T10/TG6 Volley applicability and Howling Wind interaction, Vivian counter/Trap timing, Rosa Golden Rhythm stacking, damage-taken overlap and rally-widget layering. TG6 Howling Wind's 30% chance/50% extra damage is user-reported in-game text; it does not establish other TG stages.
- Unset permanent Attack/Lethality baselines use finite sensitivity assumptions without writing them to the profile. Confirmed zero values remain zero; old unconfirmed default zeros remain unset. Sensitivity cases are not probabilities. An estimated indicator covers uncertainty capable of changing the team.
- Pet ownership follows the simplified editor's level: zero means unowned. Zero-level pets contribute no passive, refinement or active bonuses. Advancement confirmation is preserved at each milestone; completed advancement must not be suggested again. Unconfirmed advancement advice applies only if still incomplete.
- Upgrade reasons show actual documented offensive stat deltas at entered progression. Verified gear costs remain separate material quantities. Unknown hero/widget/pet costs are not verified ROI, and benefits across different resource paths have no common exchange rate. Hero-specific investments remain conditional where role selection can change; transferable gear remains separate.

- The supplementary hero review supplies primary maximum stats and community partial-star/widget tables. Gordon/Fahd/Chenko conflicting partial curves remain withheld. Marlin's two procs and Hilde's replacement-hit operation use explicitly uncertain isolated stacking cases; Margot packet/counter eligibility, Jaeger duration refresh, Triton skill-packet scope and Sophia Terror activation remain unresolved. Missing ordinary stats now mark numeric comparison readiness incomplete. Helga's owned account talent is separate from Expedition skills and counted once even when absent.
- No personal permanent-stat baseline has been recovered from a matched report. Six merged report percentages cannot identify unknown ordinary/special layers. The offline reconciliation helper rejects unknown decomposition and is not a production game formula. Existing Truegold coefficients are explicitly community estimates; unknown costs cannot establish numeric Truegold ROI.

The detailed evidence and unchanged complete profile are in [the incoming-hosting audit](../audits/incoming-hosting-2026-10-06/report.md). Remaining mechanics are release limitations to document, not a new research task.
